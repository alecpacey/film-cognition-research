01 · Foundation

The direction this research takes

Observational data gives you the index and validates TRIBE against real cortex. Generated clips are the only place you can hold five dials fixed and move one. You need both, and neither replaces the other.

The governing constraint. Every decision below follows from it.

Real cinema cannot isolate a variable, because directors covary their dials deliberately — close-ups arrive with shallow focus and quieter sound; action arrives with fast cutting and camera movement and high contrast. Kauttonen tried exactly this in 2015 with 37 cinematic features and needed elastic net to cope with the multicollinearity. You cannot separate lighting from shot scale in Pulp Fiction, because it wasn’t shot that way.

Generated clips can isolate perfectly — hold everything, move one dial — but there is no brain in the room, only a model’s prediction of one. Each track supplies precisely what the other cannot. Where they agree, the index is trustworthy.

02 · Thesis

The through-line

Build an index from cinematographic technique to cortical response profile, then invert it — specify the profile you want, and choose the technique that reaches it.

Directing with a gauge instead of an instinct.

The original hypothesis was that director’s technique would move a brain model in patterns representing an audience’s reaction, and that this would let you direct a generative model. Three claims were fused inside that sentence, and separating them is what this research did. The thesis got sharper, not smaller.

ClaimStatus
Sensor — TRIBE responds to audiovisual content in anatomically coherent ways Tested · true
V1 never enters a top-10 despite a 13× luminance range; the auditory chain climbs monotonically on speech
Control — cinematographic technique moves that response predictably The live question
Untested. Everything now turns on this
Interpretation — those responses mean something about felt experience Bounded, and removed
κ = 0.035, BF 2.3. Never load-bearing for directing

Why a profile beats an emotion word

Directing needs a target you can specify, a measurement to compare against it, and a control you can adjust. An emotion label fails all three — you cannot specify “0.7 suspense”, cannot measure it, and cannot turn it. A parcel profile succeeds at all three. It is the original idea with the unmeasurable middleman removed.

Where the emotional layer returns

Not in the loop — in the authoring. The hand-written crosswalk (suspense := 0.4·anticipation + 0.3·threat…) is a claim about what a profile means, cleanly separated from the measurement that produced it. That separation is what makes it honest rather than a dressed-up reading.

What would kill it: if technique moves no parcel reliably when varied on identical footage, the control claim fails and the thesis is dead. That test is cheap, and it comes before anything expensive.

03 · Claims

Three claims, separately true or false

The original hypothesis chains three propositions. They have very different evidential standing, and conflating them is the easiest way to build the wrong thing.

01 Technique changes TRIBE’s predicted cortical response The sensor half is now measured, not assumed. Given three 60-second clips from one film, TRIBE separated them cleanly (mean top-10 parcel overlap 0.15) and in the anatomically right direction — scenery drove ventromedial visual, talking heads drove auditory and language, a crowd drove the dorsal attention network. What remains untested is whether technique moves it as reliably as content does. Content: confirmed
02 Those patterns represent how an audience reacts Weak, and not for want of a better model. Felt emotion has no reliable measurement to validate against: seven trained annotators rating experienced emotion on the same films agreed at κ = 0.035. What survives is attention and processing — cuts drive an orienting response, event boundaries drive the hippocampus — not valence. Bounded
03 Therefore a generative model can be directed toward a target This needs only claim 1. Steering toward a target cortical profile requires the mapping to be consistent and controllable. It does not require the profile to mean anything about feeling — that interpretation sits on top and is not load-bearing. Follows from 01

Prompt adherence matters far less than it first appears. You regress on measured technique, not requested technique — so a model that renders camera movement unreliably simply hands you a messier spread, which for index-building is variation rather than error. Adherence only becomes load-bearing later, when you want to hit a target.

Both criteria met

Mean pairwise top-10 parcel overlap 0.15 against a threshold of 0.60 set beforehand — face and landscape share one parcel in ten. And the direction was right on all three clips, not the two required. Nothing told the model what it was looking at.

Output rate confirmed at exactly 1 Hz — 61 rows over 60.0 seconds. That had been open since the research phase.

Two corrections this forces

~10× slower than real time, measured — roughly 10 minutes per 60-second clip, not the 2.3–2.9× carried through the research from a ZeroGPU extrapolation. Twenty hours of film is ~200 GPU-hours, so event-locked sampling is mandatory rather than an optimisation.

Modality dropout does not buy video speed. Skipping the text branch removes ASR and Llama, but V-JEPA-2 encoding dominates — 120 steps at ~4.9 s each. The lever only works if you drop video.

The confound in this result

The face clip is two people talking, and the audio branch was live. Its top parcels are auditory and language — which may be the voices rather than the faces. The separation stands; the attribution to faces specifically does not. A silent face clip settles it, and until it does, “face” is not established as an addressable dial.

06 · The cognitive layer

From regions to meaning — how far it actually goes

The instinct is to turn an activation pattern into a feeling. There is an established method for the attempt — meta-analytic decoding, matching a pattern against thousands of published studies — and a well-quantified account of why it does not reach as far as you would want.

LayerWhat it isCan we read it?
0 · SignalPixels and waveformDirectly measured
1 · Perceptual featuresMotion energy, luminance, cut rate, face areaDirectly measured
2 · Cortical responseWhich parcels work harderYes — this is TRIBE, and this is where we operate
3 · Cognitive stateAttention, memory, threatInferred, weakly. BF ≈ 2.3
4 · ExperienceSuspense, immersion, dreadNo measurement exists. κ = 0.035

The useful accident is that cinematography also operates at layer 2. A cinematographer does not manipulate suspense directly; they manipulate what is in frame, how large, how lit, how fast it moves and what you hear. Those are stimulus properties, and stimulus properties are exactly what early and mid cortex responds to reliably. The craft and the instrument meet on the same layer. Layers 3 and 4 sit downstream of both, and inferring them adds error without adding control.

What we can say

Stimulus-content readings. “Structured audio present.” “Scene-biased ventral visual.” “Object form present.” These are supportable from activation because the regions carrying them are close to the input and strongly selective.

Our probe returned three whole documented chains, not isolated parcels: the complete acoustic-to-social pathway (PBelt → A4 → A5 → STSdp) on the talking clip, five of seven attention parcels on the crowd, and parahippocampal place cortex on the landscape. Chains are far harder to produce by noise or misalignment than single regions.

What we cannot

Cognitive nouns. The best-quantified reverse inference in the field — Broca’s area to language — is a posterior of 0.69 at Bayes factor 2.3, and BF below 4 is weak. Under a realistic 15% prior it falls to P = 0.29.

FFA and PPA are not exempt. “There is no ‘face’ identification area” — neither is uniquely diagnostic for its preferred class. The distinction that survives is stimulus versus cognition, not region versus region.

Two traps that would produce confident fiction

Neurosynth’s vocabulary is uncurated abstract tokens — 3,228 of them, 713 multi-word, 60 beginning with a digit, including 12 healthy and voxel. Every filmmaker-facing word is absent or a homograph of methods prose: presence means presence of a lesion, flow means blood flow, character means letters on a screen. A pipeline decoding to it would report “high presence” while measuring English prose habits. Use NeuroQuery’s psychology-categorised vocabulary (1,604 terms) instead.

Manipulation-strength bias predicts your results before you run them. Term maps are built from strong isolated lab manipulations; film is weak, continuous and confounded. Terms with strong source paradigms — pain, faces, motion — will outrank terms with weak ones — empathy, suspense, social inference — regardless of what the film does. Yarkoni: “I’m not sure there’s any good way to correct for this.”

And the failure is fluent, not loud. Four coordinates millimetres apart inside one region returned entirely different confident term lists — 86% experiencing, 85% abuse, 84% dysregulation. A mislocalised parcel does not error; it tells you a different, equally plausible story.

The design that survives

Decode contrasts, never single shots — close-up versus wide of the same scene, cut versus no-cut at matched content. Meta-analytic decoding is informative for matched contrasts and useless for single-condition maps, and one shot’s map is a single-condition map.

Spin-test every correlation. Naive nulls “consistently yield unrealistically liberal statistical estimates”; without a spatial-autocorrelation- preserving null, every term looks significant.

The layer you have to author yourself

Roughly two-thirds of directorial intent is decodable — attention, threat, arousal, valence, empathy, mentalizing, agency, surprise, memory encoding, spatial cognition, narrative. Absent from all six vocabularies checked: suspense, tension, dread, immersion, presence, dramatic irony. (tension exists in NeuroQuery; it resolves to hypotension.)

The constructs have neuroimaging correlates — they simply never entered the ontologies, because communication scholars and cognitive neuroscientists do not share a vocabulary. So the top layer is hand-authored, roughly thirty terms shaped like suspense := 0.4·anticipation + 0.3·threat + 0.2·uncertainty + 0.1·vigilance. A week of careful work, and the only part nobody can download for you.

Practical notes for the build: there is no canonical volumetric Glasser and no fsaverage_to_mni152 in neuromaps, so the reference matrix must be brought down to fsaverage5 rather than pushing parcels up. Use CAB-NP for network assignments — Yeo7 has no language network and mislabels four of our best parcels. Demean each parcel column across terms before correlating, or every clip returns the same ranking. And neuromaps is CC BY-NC-SA, which stacks with TRIBE’s licence.

07 · Sources

Which databases, and what each costs you

The decisive question for every dataset is not size or prestige. It is whether it can feed TRIBE at all — which needs continuous video on a window of at least 30 seconds. That single requirement eliminates most of the cinematography corpora, which are still-frame collections.

The pairing does not exist, and that is the gap worth occupying

No public dataset annotates both technique and audience response at usable scale. The affect corpora treat the film as an opaque stimulus; the technique corpora never collect a viewer. CineScale’s own authors name shot-scale-to-emotion as their motivation and then collect no affect labels. Constructing that pairing — even at small scale, even with a model standing in for the audience — is the novel contribution here.

09 · Instruments

Measuring what actually came out

Pixels, not models

cinemetrics.py measures camera motion, cuts, lighting key, colour and depth of field from image statistics alone — 2 seconds per clip, no GPU. Verified here on clips its author never saw: push-in detected at net_zoom 1.44 against a truth of 1.5, pan correct, 2 of 2 real cuts found, and zero false cuts on continuous motion.

That last figure is the important one. A fixed-threshold detector reported 71 cuts in a 72-frame single-take pan — camera motion alone exceeds the default threshold every frame.

Why not a vision model

Across 24 VLMs on ShotBench, GPT-4o averages 59.3%, half the field is under 50%, and camera movement is described as approaching random. The purpose-built ShotVL scores 62.9% on movement — worse than the optical-flow baseline you can run for free.

Add exactly one model, Vchitect/ShotVL-7B, for camera angle and lighting type — the only two dials image statistics genuinely cannot reach.

10 · Silent failures

Four ways this measures nothing and looks fine

01

Activations are ~50× smaller than intuition

A natural face photograph drives the fusiform face area to +0.080 z-units; even a gradient-ascent-optimised stimulus reaches only +0.339. Any threshold written by intuition never fires, and every condition returns the same answer.

Fix: thresholds in rolling-sigma units, with a floor calibrated from logged per-region standard deviation — roughly its 10th percentile.

02

Windows under 30 s return garbage without erroring

TRIBE was trained on 100-second windows. Below about 30 seconds the output goes diffuse and low-intensity — and the floor is enforced nowhere in code. This is the constraint that eliminates every still-frame dataset and threatens short-clip corpora.

Fix: score rolling 30 s windows. Long-form film gives them free; clip corpora may not be usable at all.

03

TRIBE barely saw cinema

Of the 121 hours of video it ever encountered, 64.5 hours is Friends — a multi-camera sitcom with almost no cinematographic variation. Only three of its eight datasets contain real cinema. A model trained mostly on a sitcom may not be sensitive to the dials in question.

Fix: this is precisely what the observational track tests. It is also why Friends-adjacent evaluation is circular, and why held-out StudyForrest matters.

04

Multicollinearity in real film

Directors covary dials on purpose. Regressing 37 cinematic features against fMRI required elastic net for exactly this reason. A naive regression will happily attribute an editing effect to shot scale.

Fix: penalised regression on the observational track, and the generated track as the orthogonal control. This is the whole argument for two tracks.

11 · Output

The experiment decides which film

A film comes out of this either way. Which one depends on whether the index holds.

If the index holds — the controller

Scenes directed toward target cortical profiles. Set a trajectory across the runtime and have the system steer toward it, shot by shot. This is the original hypothesis realised: a simulated viewer in the director’s chair, and the first film cut to a measured neural target rather than a guess about one.

If it doesn’t — the collapse

Run the maximiser instead and film what it does. Told to drive the cortex as hard as possible, it narrows from landscape to face over ten minutes and then holds, at a measured endpoint of 61–70% face purity. It needs no control law — only the drift — and the degeneration is the argument.

Either way the cortex stays on screen beside the picture, and either way the honest register is perceptual. You can say this is the configuration a simulated visual system enters. You cannot say this is what the audience feels — not because the model is inadequate, but because felt emotion has no reliable measurement to validate against.

12 · Open

What to settle first, cheapest first

Does TRIBE discriminate content at all? Three videos you already own — a landscape, a crowd, a face. If the top regions do not separate them, nothing downstream works. Free, and it should be the first thing run.
Does it respond to cut rate on identical footage? The cleanest possible test — same material, one variable, nothing passing through a generator. Also the dial with the largest effect in the literature, and plausibly the one TRIBE's sitcom-heavy training covers best.
Does TRIBE's prediction correlate with real BOLD on cinema? The feasibility question for the whole programme. Score StudyForrest's film, correlate against the measured response of 15 people who watched it in a scanner, per cortical region. Public data, GPU cost only. If it fails, the directed-film hypothesis is dead and you know before spending on anything else.
Does the face response survive without speech? The probe's face clip is two people talking, and its top parcels are auditory and language. So the response may be to the voices, not the faces. A silent face clip separates them — cheap, and it decides whether "face" is even a dial we can address.
How much of NNDb needs scoring At the measured ~10× real time, twenty hours of film is ~200 GPU-hours, roughly $200 on an A10G. Event-locked sampling — 30 s windows around annotated technique changes — is what makes this affordable rather than a nice-to-have. Design the sampling before renting anything.
